    How a project runs

    Four steps, one designer, and no surprises on price.

    1. 1

      Free consultation

      A 30-minute call or visit. We ask how you live, what you want to keep and what you can't stand.

    2. 2

      Concept board

      Within a week you see palette, materials, layout and mood images for your space.

    3. 3

      Quote and refine

      We send a fixed quote and rework the design until it feels right. Two revision rounds are included.

    4. 4

      Build and style

      We order, coordinate the trades, oversee the install and style the room on the final day.

    Questions we hear often

    How much does a project cost?

    Every service lists a starting price. After your consultation we send a fixed quote in writing, and we don't order anything until you approve it.

    Can I start with just a consultation?

    Yes. Book a consultation on its own and decide what to do next afterwards. There's no obligation to continue.

    Can you work with my builder or contractor?

    Yes. We can work with your team or bring in trusted trades. Either way we give them clear drawings and stay available during the install.

    Do you work remotely?

    Yes. Concept boards, 3D views and sourcing all work over video and WhatsApp. Site visits are best for measuring and for install day.

    What if I don't like the concept?

    Each design service includes two revision rounds. Tell us what's off and we rework the board until it feels right.

    How long does a room take?

    Design usually takes two to three weeks. Delivery and install depend on furniture lead times, and we put dates in your quote.
